=== Commit 1 ===
WORHP: fix iteration_callback never being called (#4053)

The callback was guarded by `if (!firstIteration)` with firstIteration
initialised to true and never reset, so it was dead code. Evaluate the
callback at every iterOutput action and handle errors like the Ipopt
interface (nonzero return, KeyboardInterrupt, ignore_errors option).
